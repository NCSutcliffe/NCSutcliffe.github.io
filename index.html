About

I'm a Technical Product Owner specialising in Modern Workplace Security within a large enterprise environment. My focus is adoption product management — intake, prioritisation, stakeholder alignment, and change communication for Microsoft security feature rollouts. I care about the gap between "it's deployed" and "it's actually working."

I hold a PGDip in Advanced Security and Digital Forensics and a BSc in Digital Forensics. Outside the day job, I write and create content on security usability and thought leadership across the Microsoft security stack.
